# S4. Painting, pedigree inference and founder refit

## Painting

Parameter names follow the generated reference; values are 4.4.0 defaults; method units are in parentheses.

**Alphabet, bins and local keys.** Classes are exact call signatures, numbered by first appearance; unanchored classes and BACKGROUND share one public UNKNOWN identifier (PR01). Bins split the $L_s$ selected sites into $\max(1,\lfloor L_s/\texttt{snps\_per\_bin}\rfloor)$ runs, with `snps_per_bin` = 100; the first $L_s \bmod B$ runs are one site longer, independently of task windows or sample batches (PR02). In each bin, anchored classes called there with identical $\{-1,0,1\}$ vectors share their smallest index as a key; other anchored classes key to themselves, and the rest to UNKNOWN. Local equality does not imply chromosome-wide row identity (PR03).

**Emissions and transitions.** A site is informative when it is observed and its likelihood row spread exceeds `INFORMATIVE_SPREAD` = $16\varepsilon_{\mathrm{mach}}$. Sums run in site order and are divided once by the design effect. A bin split by label continuity keeps its whole-bin denominator in every part (PR04). `MINIMUM_SWITCH_PROBABILITY` = $10^{-15}$. The switch probability $\theta_b$ never exceeds $(S-1)/S$, and edges of zero length are left out of the $\rho$ update. The rate $\rho$ is a pooled copying rate, not a count of meioses (PR05). Track states are the $S$ faithful states followed by one error state per anchored class, $S'\le 2K+1$; the first bin draws labels uniformly and fidelity from the stationary law. Painting reads every sample, including those QC excludes from founder construction. The diploid transition factorises over tracks, so recursions cost $O(S'^2)$ per bin and sample (PR06). Forward–backward is scaled at every step and reduced in sample order (PR07).

**Estimator.** Starting values are `initial_switch_rate` = 2.0, `initial_error_entry` = 0.001 and `initial_error_exit` = 0.25, with `estimation_iterations` = 8 updates (PR08). With $n_{\mathrm{tr}}$ eligible tracks and expected label changes $\bar n_b\le n_{\mathrm{tr}}$, the update for $\rho$ maximises

$$
Q(\rho)=\sum_{b:\,d_b>0}\Big[\bar n_b\log\theta_b(\rho)+(n_{\mathrm{tr}}-\bar n_b)\log\big(1-\theta_b(\rho)\big)\Big]
$$

over `RATE_BOUNDS` = (0.01, 1000) per Morgan. The search takes `RATE_GRID_POINTS` = 241 log-spaced points, then `GOLDEN_STEPS` = 60 golden-section steps. The previous value is kept unless $Q$ improves; $Q$ can be multimodal, so the search is not a certificate. Let $n_{\uparrow}$ and $n_{\downarrow}$ be the expected error-run starts and ends, $k_f$ and $k_e$ the kept faithful and kept error transitions, and $\iota_e$ and $\iota_f$ the stationary draws into error and faithful states. The update for $(\alpha,\beta)$ maximises

$$
(n_{\uparrow}+\iota_e)\log\alpha+k_f\log(1-\alpha)+(n_{\downarrow}+\iota_f)\log\beta+k_e\log(1-\beta)-(\iota_e+\iota_f)\log(\alpha+\beta)
$$

over `ENTRY_BOUNDS` = $(10^{-6},0.5)$ and `EXIT_BOUNDS` = $(10^{-3},0.999)$. It uses L-BFGS-B in log space from the previous values and from stay-only ratios, keeping the previous values unless improved. Results are clipped and rounded to a natural-log grid of `estimate_grid` = $2^{-6}$. Without an anchored class there is no error state, and $(\alpha,\beta)$ are reported at their bounds. A fitted $\alpha=10^{-6}$ is a boundary fit, not a test of absence.

**Decoding.** Predecessors within `VITERBI_TIE` = $2^{-15}$ nats of the best are tied. Ties prefer staying in the same state, then the same label in its other fidelity state, then a label change from the smallest index (the largest under reversal); the final state is the smallest index within the quantum (PR09). At ties between ordered and swapped continuations, the suffix is swapped if the swap gives strictly fewer allele-visible changes. A change between classes that share a local key is not allele-visible, and a pure swap is no switch (PR10). Switch intervals use a discrete test on calls, the observed mask and the shared-allele rule, and stop at neighbouring switches. The two conventions' masks are combined into the smaller unordered cover. Equal-size covers that differ give every candidate to both tracks. Canonical track order is fixed right to left by fewer changes, then fewer allele-visible changes, then the smaller mask on track 0. `MAXIMUM_MASK_STATES` = 63 requires $S\le63$; at $K=30$, $S\le31$ (PR11).

**Release.** Only $q^{F}$ and $q^{AF}$ are computed, each the minimum over the two conventions (PR12). The threshold `minimum_release_posterior` = 0.9 is inclusive, with no tie band. Statuses take precedence in this order: ineligible, low posterior, switch interval, local equivalence, then the class status. Member sets are the class's rows (singleton or pooled), the rows of all key-sharing classes (local equivalence), or the union of the candidates' rows, extended to key-sharing classes in stage-2 bins and empty if any candidate is BACKGROUND or unanchored (switch interval). A switch interval cannot release a bin that failed the staged rule, and ineligible samples report zero switches (PR13); caching affects resources only (PR14).

**Label continuity.** Link points are the first selected sites of atomic spans. Only singleton anchored classes move, and the search needs two movable classes, a link and two bins (PR15). Exact transposition gains separate the unaffected states from the $4S-4$ affected ones, using left and right max-product messages, and are summed in sample order (PR16). The unit $\Delta_{\mathrm{sw}}$ is the median over edges of the log-probability of the ordered pair staying minus that of one specified single-track change. Candidates gaining at least `label_continuity_minimum_switch_gain` = 2 units of $\Delta_{\mathrm{sw}}$ are ranked in descending tie classes. The `nll` tie quantum is the smallest power of two at least $\max(2^{-10},2^{-20}\times\text{largest gain})$ nats. A class wider than `WIDE_CLASS_QUANTA` = 4 quanta keeps value order; otherwise link and pair index decide. A placement moves left while the two rows carry identical founder-axis data between consecutive links.

Acceptance requires the painted-switch total to fall by at least 2, with losers at most `label_continuity_maximum_loser_ratio` = 0.1 times winners. A tie class is skipped when another acceptable member would relabel differently, and a candidate that changes a class's error capability is rejected. Each accepted move lowers the total, ensuring termination (PR17). Verification requires the relabelled panel to have identical classes, sites and anchoring and a strictly lower repainted total; otherwise the assembled panel is kept, with a recorded reason (PR18). The stage order is model, continuity, decode, release (PR19).

**Cost.** The chain costs $O(NBS'^2)$, masks at worst $O(NBS^3)$, and the screen $O(\text{links}\,NS^3)$ per iteration. Label-continuity checks have an $O(K^4)$ worst case, carried as documented exception 0e (PR17). Tie peers are grouped once per iteration; each tested candidate can still require a full decode.

## Pedigree scoring

**Inputs.** A candidate is available in a component when it has an observed site with likelihood spread above `INFORMATIVE_GL_SPREAD` = $10^{-12}$. An unavailable parent reduces M1 to M0, and M2 to the other parent's raw M1 (PS01). At most `markers_per_bin` = 10 markers are kept per painting bin: all of them, or $\text{first}+\lfloor s\,n/10\rfloor$ for $s=0,\dots,9$. A component is admitted when some selected likelihood triple is non-uniform, and anchoring precedes subsampling (PS02). An information group closes once its running count of selected markers reaches `markers_per_information_block` = 100, with each empty bin counted as one. Then $\eta=\min(3,n)/n$, from `effective_markers_per_information_block` = 3, and $\eta=0$ without informative markers (PS03). The selector probability $r_b$ uses model distances, fallback tails included, clipped to $[10^{-15},0.5]$ (`SELECTOR_SWITCH_FLOOR`). Measured lengths are clipped to the map's knots unless `rate_is_measured` (default false), and are zero without a map (PS04).

**Projection.** Within the source chain only the initial law is symmetrised. Evidence-free samples take a uniform initial law and unit right weights, and a loss of supported mass is an invariant error (PS05). The projection then symmetrises each bin's marginal. BACKGROUND keeps its unconditioned ALT probability when the conditioning denominator is not positive (PS06). Each bridge is $\operatorname{diag}(\text{stay})$ plus a rank-one off-diagonal term and at most one pivot arm. Its maximum-entropy root is found by at most `bridge_max_iterations` = 128 bisection halvings. A post-check (`bridge_postcheck_tolerance` = $2\times10^{-13}$) verifies it; failure stops the stage, with no fallback (PS07). The excursion weight is $\upsilon_b=r_b\,(1-\min\{1,n/\texttt{source\_gap\_support\_sites}\})$. Here `source_gap_support_sites` = 1 and $n$ is the smaller adjacent-bin informative-marker count (PS08). `null_prior_samples` = 1. A rounding-sized negative null persistence saturates to the uniform kernel; a genuinely negative one raises an error (PS09).

**Child forward.** `mismatch_probability` = 0.01. Underflowing bin products are recomputed in log space. Tempering is $\mathrm{pow}(\mathcal E/\max\mathcal E,\eta)$ for a bin emission $\mathcal E$; the power is skipped at $\eta=1$, and $\eta=0$ still normalises. The composite weight $\tfrac12$ is fixed (PS10).

**Certified M1 and the panel.** Block-certificate bounds keep called alleles exact, relax uncertain ones to endpoint likelihoods and take the smaller of two block partitions, with outward padding (PS11). Exact queries cover, in order, the tie class of maximal bounds, the best frontier, the floor frontier and every cell able to reach a local maximum. With chromosome temperatures $t_z=\max(\lceil M_z/100\rceil,1)^{1/2}$ (`information_tempering_power` = 0.5), let $t(M)$ be the same function of the genome total of selected markers, $M$. A candidate is retained when its genome M1 reaches $\chi_c=\max\text{M1}+\texttt{LOG\_FLOOR}\cdot t(M)$, with `LOG_FLOOR` = $-60$. All others receive

$$
\ell^{\mathrm{floor}}_{cz}=\hat\ell_{cz}-\frac{t_z}{\sum_{z'}t_{z'}}\Big(\sum_{z'}\hat\ell_{cz'}-\chi_c\Big),
$$

where $\hat\ell_{cz}$ is the certified chromosome-local maximum. The floor is applied exactly once, because it is not idempotent (PS12). Screen utilities decide nothing (PS13).

The pair bound gives every state with $\pi\ge$ `pair_bound_high_mass` = 0.01 its own group, gives BACKGROUND its own group, and puts all remaining named states in one low group. Emission envelopes use the corners of the transmitted-ALT box, since the site likelihood is bilinear (PS14). A pair enters the panel if, in the full fit or any LOCO fit $w$, $w\cdot\text{bound}\ge\max(w\cdot\text{M0},\max_{q} w\cdot\text{M1}_{q})-\texttt{pair\_relevance\_margin}$, $q$ retained, with margin 150, or if the gap is NaN. There is no cap on parents or pairs (PS15). In a component where only one parent of a pair is available, the pair scores as that parent's M1 (PS16).

**Exposure and junctions.** Exposed map length integrates the smaller of the two adjacent bins' exposures over capped measured intervals (PS17). Junction counts come from an exact minimum-switch dynamic programme over unordered pairs of deduplicated trajectories, with costs 0, 1 and 2. Unknown labels are compatible with every trajectory, and callable bins count non-negative labels on both tracks (PS18). The schedule traces the first minimum path, or the last under reversal. Detection is $\min(1,\text{callable}/2B)$. Counts above the child's total are dropped, and zero retained mass gives $-\infty$. Equally parsimonious alternative placements are not integrated (PS19).

**Exclusion e-value.** At a jointly observed site $j$, with normalised likelihood triples $\boldsymbol\xi$ and $\boldsymbol\zeta$, define $\Psi_j=\max\{\xi_0\max(\zeta_0,\zeta_1),\,\xi_1\max_g \zeta_g,\,\xi_2\max(\zeta_1,\zeta_2)\}$ and $\Omega_j=\max_g \xi_g\max_g \zeta_g$. The site ratio is

$$
\mathcal R_j=\frac{(\xi_0\zeta_2+\xi_2\zeta_0)/2}{\max\{\Psi_j,(1-\delta)\Psi_j+\delta\,\Omega_j\}},\qquad \delta=\texttt{genotype\_replacement\_probability}=0.01.
$$

For chromosome $z$, $E_z$ averages $\prod_{j\in \omega}(1-\nu+\nu \mathcal R_j)$ over `bet_sizes` $\nu\in\{2^{-10},2^{-8},2^{-6},2^{-4},2^{-2},1\}$ and over windows $\omega$. The windows are the whole contig and half-overlapping windows of `window_widths_bp` = 1, 5 and 20 Mb. $E$ is the mean of $E_z$ over all configured contigs (PS20). A completed-window lower bound may certify a dyad early, and a threshold-straddling interval raises an error (PS21).

## Pedigree decision and release

**Evidence scale.** Let $\mathcal S^{\mathrm{tr}}_{cf\mathsf a}$ and $\mathcal S^{\mathrm{ho}}_{cf\mathsf a}$ be the training and held-out log-likelihood sums of configuration $\mathsf a$ for fold $f$. Let $\varpi_{c\mathsf a}=\log\Pi_{s(\mathsf a)}-\log n_{s(\mathsf a)}$, with $n_s$ the eligible configurations in state $s$. Let $m_{cf\mathsf a}\in\{0,-\infty\}$ exclude reciprocal-dominant rows: those naming $p$ as $c$'s parent with $\mathrm{M1}(p\mid c)-\mathrm{M0}(p)\ge\mathrm{M1}(c\mid p)-\mathrm{M0}(c)$, which genetics cannot order. The objective is

$$
F(\lambda)=\sum_c\sum_{f=0}^{2}\left[\log\sum_{\mathsf a} e^{\lambda\mathcal S^{\mathrm{tr}}_{cf\mathsf a}+\varpi_{c\mathsf a}+m_{cf\mathsf a}+\mathcal S^{\mathrm{ho}}_{cf\mathsf a}}-\log\sum_{\mathsf a} e^{\lambda\mathcal S^{\mathrm{tr}}_{cf\mathsf a}+\varpi_{c\mathsf a}+m_{cf\mathsf a}}\right].
$$

Folds are chromosome index modulo 4, with at least two chromosomes per fold. The search evaluates a 33-point grid of $\log\lambda$ on $[\log0.001,\log100]$ and refines every eligible interior maximum. All optimisations must succeed, and ties go to the value closest to $\lambda=1$. The scale is applied only when the gain exceeds $10^{-8}$, the optimum lies at least 0.01 from a bound and $\lambda<1$. Fold 3's test gain is only reported; `evidence_calibration` = "off" sets $\lambda=1$ (PD03).

**Likelihoods and gate.** Configuration rows are ordered as M0, then M1 by ascending parent, then canonical panel M2 (PD01–PD02). The M0 mixture uses `m0_contamination_probability` = 0.02 and is applied once (PD04). The gate thresholds are `minimum_edge_exposed_bins` = `minimum_pair_exposed_bins` = 1, `minimum_exposed_fraction` = 0.1 and `minimum_exposed_units` = 3, with `unit_morgans` = 1.0. Units are $\max(1,\text{length}/\texttt{unit\_morgans})$, or 1 for a non-finite length. A bootstrap-repeated chromosome counts once. An untestable row keeps the M0 state score and gets an identity score of $-\infty$ (PD05).

**Direction and family terms.** For a directional log-CDF difference $\Delta_{pc}\ne0$, with $\sigma$ the logistic function, chromosome log odds are $\log[(1-\epsilon_{\mathrm{chr}})\sigma(\Delta_{pc})+\epsilon_{\mathrm{chr}}/2]-\log[(1-\epsilon_{\mathrm{chr}})\sigma(-\Delta_{pc})+\epsilon_{\mathrm{chr}}/2]$, with `chromosome_error_probability` $\epsilon_{\mathrm{chr}}$ = 0.05 (PD06). The configuration term is $\log2+\log[(1-\epsilon_{\mathrm{pair}})\sigma(O)+\epsilon_{\mathrm{pair}}/2]$, where $O$ is the summed log odds and `pair_failure_probability` $\epsilon_{\mathrm{pair}}$ = 0.02. A junction-density order probability, a retained heuristic, only orders graph visits, though this can decide which child of a cycle is displaced (`order_minimum_weight` = 2, `order_variance_offset` = 1; PD07). There are `message_passes` = 4 preparatory cavity passes. Rows naming $p$ as $c$'s parent receive $-\log(1+e^{O^{\mathrm{cav}}_{pc}})$, where $O^{\mathrm{cav}}_{pc}$ is the cavity log odds that $c$ is $p$'s parent (PD08). The path factor uses `ancestry_path_budget` = 16, moves a boundary tie class that would overfill the panel to the tail, and is then frozen (PD09).

The final solve runs undamped to `final_tolerance` = $10^{-8}$ within `final_max_iterations` = 8192, with progress checks every 512 iterations. A stalled solve is retried damped (`retry_iterations` = 4096, `retry_damping` = 0.5) and then resumed. Non-convergence is fatal for the full fit; a resample abstains but stays in the denominator. Release direction log odds are cavity log-odds differences (PD10).

**Winners, graph and priors.** Two scores tie when their difference is at most $\max(512\,\varepsilon_{\mathrm{mach}}\max(1,|\text{score}|),2^{-15})$, under `contrast_ulp_factor` and `noise_floor`. Row index never breaks a tie (PD11). The decision score is the marginal state score plus the identity score minus the state's best identity score, and unresolved-state and unresolved-identity reasons stay distinct (PD12). The acyclic step peels cyclic components; tied weakest children become graph-tie conflicts. Greedy selection then visits children by order probability, confidence and index, and keeps the local row when it is feasible. Otherwise it takes the unique feasible winner from the local state downwards, over `graph_local_search_passes` = 3 passes. This is not global graph optimisation (PD13). The `prior_sensitivity` reselection reuses the family terms and is not repeated in resamples (PD14).

**Units and resampling.** A chromosome is informative when the child's scaled per-chromosome state evidence, without family terms, has a unique winner. That winner is not required to match the selected state. The chromosome contributes the largest exposed units of the child's eligible edges, or 1 for a child without edges (PD15). Bootstrap uses `bootstrap_replicates` = 1000 draws from a PCG64 stream with `bootstrap_seed` = 20260725. Identical vectors are fitted once and counted by multiplicity. Integer counts make support independent of scheduling (PD16). A precheck needs $N\ge3$ and 3 informative contigs, and reports attainable support and each tier's minimum contig count (PD17).

**Tiers and views.** The thresholds (configuration bootstrap, parent bootstrap, LOCO) are `tier_b` = (0.7, 0.7, 0.8) and `tier_a` = (0.95, 0.95, 0.95). The direction cutoff is $\log(0.98/0.02)$, from `orientation_reversal_probability` = 0.02. An exact configuration also needs no graph-tie conflict and, for M0, a graph row that stays M0 under every prior. Unresolved statuses rank prior veto, no stable parent, insufficient support; resolved ones rank graph-tie conflict, graph conflict, unresolved direction, unresolved identity (PD18). Displaced rows of the complete view are never tier-eligible (PD19). A marginal edge needs the same parent in the local and graph rows, both parent supports, no graph conflict and enough units, but the state itself need not pass (PD20). Candidate sets cover `support_set_coverage` = 0.95 of the within-state softmax of decision scores, completing tie classes at a quantum of $2^{-15}$ (PD21).

**Counts, guard and slots.** Count-release targets are Tier-B children with an unresolved state or fewer named parents than called. The threshold is $\log(2N/\alpha_{\mathrm E})$, with `alpha` $\alpha_{\mathrm E}$ = 0.01. Release needs every candidate scored, enough units, no graph conflict, at most one survivor, agreement with the supported and complete parents, and no withheld edge, and is labelled as exclusion-based evidence (PD22). The guard uses `minimum_family` = 3, so at least two tested members, and `member_support` = 0.75. It contracts the edge set to a fixed point in at most $E+1$ passes, runs count release with the withheld set, and verifies both sets. Guarded views clear the affected calls but keep their candidate lists (PD23). Slots order parents by descending observed-site counts, compared contig by contig, with names breaking exact ties (PD24). Relative-proxy contrasts are diagnostics that no release reads (PD26).

**Cost.** Sources cost $O(NBS^2)$, transmitted ALT $O(NMS)$, the pair bound $O(R^2BG^3)$ for $R$ retained candidates and $G\le S+1$, and exposure $O((E/2+T)BS(S+1)/2)$ for $T$ admitted pairs. The decision has no intrinsic $K$ term and runs at most $1000+C$ fits.

## Founder allele refit

Evidence uses the founder-construction view, so QC-flagged samples are unobserved. A sample scores at a site only when both of its tracks carry released singleton rows, and sites with no scoring sample are unchanged (FR01). Each site gets `coordinate_sweeps` = 3 sweeps. A sweep has a flip phase, then a two-row phase in which swaps and pair flips are restricted to co-painted rows. A move qualifies when its gain is positive and at least `minimum_gain_nats` = 5 per changed cell. A round applies qualifying moves in decision order, skipping any that share a row, or a row co-painted with one of theirs, with a move already applied. A row changes at most once per phase and sweep, and a sweep without changes ends the site. Qualification and rechecks use the same direct two-row gain (FR02).

Tie classes chain gains within $\max(2^{-16},(n_i+n_j)2^{-18})$ nats, where $n$ counts scoring carriers (`REFIT_TIE_QUANTUM`, `REFIT_TIE_QUANTUM_PER_CARRIER`). A class wider than `WIDE_QUANTA` = 4 quanta keeps value order; otherwise row or pair index decides. Reversal reorders a class but never changes its membership (FR03). Interacting candidates in one tie class are applied hypothetically in both orders; if either then fails to qualify, all rows involved become unknown in one pass, the round is rescored, and those alleles cannot be filled later (FR04). A fill applies only to alleles unknown in the painted panel. It needs a gain of 5 nats and at least `fill_min_supporters` = 2 carriers whose reads favour the allele by more than $2^{-16}$. Fills are decided against the post-sweep state and applied together (FR05). The refit panel goes to family phase and export; pedigree keeps the pre-refit founders (FR06). The cost per site and sweep is $O(N+K^3\log K)$. In the worst case, two-row gains cost $O(K^4)$, and tie resolution $O(K^4)$ flips and $O(K^5)$ swaps per phase. These analytic worst cases remain under exception 0e (FR04).


## Published tables

Painting writes the run-level tables `painting/sample_segments.csv`, `sample_switches.csv`,
`founder_support.csv`, `founder_support_windows.csv`, `founder_counts.csv`,
`label_continuity.csv`, `painting_model.csv` and `assembly_search.csv`, each with a leading
`contig` column. The segment table uses `sample`, `component`, `track`, `start`, `end`, `status`,
`label` and `rows`; relatedness and pedigree tables likewise use snake_case names such as
`sample`, `parent1`, `parent2`, `parent_state` and `release_evidence`, and retain their run-level
paths. These table keys identify inferred output rows and carry no biological parentage truth.
